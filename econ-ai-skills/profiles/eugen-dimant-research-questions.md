## #1: Do Humans Adopt Social Norms from AI Agents?

**Target:** Science, Nature Human Behaviour, AER

### The Puzzle
Your work establishes that humans are profoundly influenced by peer behavior and social norms. But the "peers" people interact with are increasingly artificial: ChatGPT, recommendation algorithms, AI assistants. **Do people treat AI-generated behavioral suggestions as normative information?** If someone believes "the AI recommends X," does this carry the same weight as "most people do X"?

### Why This Is Top-Journal Material
- **Novelty:** First rigorous experimental examination of AI-to-human norm transmission
- **Timeliness:** AI adoption is accelerating; policymakers urgently need to understand behavioral implications
- **Your Comparative Advantage:** You are among the world's leading experts on norm measurement and behavioral contagion—you have the methodological toolkit others lack
- **Identification:** Clean experimental designs comparing AI vs. human normative information

### Proposed Approach
1. **Experiment 1:** Norm elicitation task where subjects receive descriptive norm information from (a) "other participants," (b) "AI recommendation," (c) "algorithm based on past behavior." Measure behavioral contagion.
2. **Experiment 2:** Honesty paradigm (your expertise) where AI provides implicit normative cues about acceptable dishonesty levels.
3. **Mechanism exploration:** Is it credibility? Perceived expertise? Reduced social accountability?

### Key Contribution
Establish whether AI constitutes a new channel of norm transmission—with profound implications for how algorithmic recommendations shape societal behavior at scale.

- **Policy relevance:** Policymakers need to know if behavioral interventions are worth the investment
